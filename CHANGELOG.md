# v2026.4

- Contractors: a new Contractors page keeps each contractor's details, W-9 and payments. It also makes 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC forms, saves IRIS upload files, tracks TIN matching and IRS B notices, and withholds backup withholding when required.
- Accountant package: new year-end package with journal entries, a payroll register, what's still owed, contractor payments, estimated taxes and possible tax credits. It now adds up line by line, with bookmarks and a contents page in the PDF.
- Corrections: record a correction to an earlier quarter, and filed Forms 941, 940 and W-2s now show what has changed since they were filed and which form fixes it (941-X, 940, W-2c).
- Add files: drop a W-4, I-9 or W-9 anywhere in the app and it is read, filed with the right person, and used to fill in their details. Scans and photos are read with built-in text recognition. Completed forms are kept encrypted.
- Employees: pages are split into tabs. New options cover HSA and FSA contributions, taxable non-cash benefits, expense reimbursements, and a single "Kind of work" list for workers who are exempt from certain taxes.
- 401(k): company contributions beyond the match, 2026 limit checks, a "true-up match" option, and clearer deposit deadlines. You can also choose no plan, a SIMPLE IRA or a SEP-IRA.
- Pay stubs, W-2s and forms: pay stubs show more year-to-date detail, W-2s follow the 2026 IRS layout, and Forms 941, 940 and 945 can be marked final, with a third-party designee.
- Notices and checklists: required posters and notices were expanded for many states, with a new "If you close the business" section. Home, Money to send, Paydays and Tax forms now share one checklist style.
- State updates: withholding, unemployment, paid leave, minimum wage, final pay and payday rules were updated for many states and cities, including pay after an employee's death and military spouses.
- Home has a search box, and Settings has a Text size choice with View → Zoom In and Zoom Out.
- Sole proprietors get quarterly estimated tax reminders, an Owner's draws page and a simpler setup. S corporation owners get reasonable-wage warnings.
- Windows and Linux now store the encryption key and email password in the system's secure store. You can print a recovery code page.

# v2026.3

- File → Export All Data… saves everything in your company file as spreadsheet (CSV) files in a zip.
- Switching from QuickBooks Online or Desktop? You can now import your employees and this year's paychecks from QuickBooks reports.
- Help → Show Log File… shows a troubleshooting log you can attach to an email when asking for help. It never contains Social Security numbers, email addresses or pay amounts.
- On a Mac, "Update to…" now installs the new version for you and reopens Counting Carrots.
- People using a shared company file from a web browser can now download tables and pay stubs as PDFs or spreadsheets.

# v2026.2

- Home now shows one checklist of everything to do: money to send, tax forms to file, notices and posters, and pay stubs to give. Click a row to open its steps, and click the circle to mark it done.
- State withholding returns, yearly reconciliations, unemployment wage reports and new-hire reports are now in the Tax forms checklist with their due dates and filing steps.
- Counting Carrots can back up your company file by itself into a folder you choose, such as a cloud drive, and you can restore any backup from Settings.
- You can now email pay stubs to employees who agreed to get them that way, and Settings lists every email sent.
- Each form and payment has a box for your own notes, and Settings is split into General, Email and Backups tabs.
- The app now remembers its window size and position, and many smaller fixes and wording improvements.
